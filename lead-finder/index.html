Jader website ache: report pathan

  1. Tool site ta check kore problem ber kore. Email e "First email" copy kore pathan: 3 ta problem ar free report er offer.
  2. Reply dile Report khulun, Save as PDF kore "Send report" email er sathe pathan. Report e lekha thake keno redesign dorkar.
  3. Client sample dekhte chaile Redesign prompt khulun: screenshot download korun, prompt copy kore Claude e paste korun, screenshot attach korun.
  4. Claude er design er screenshot ba link "Send sample redesign" email e pathan. Dam call e ba nijer moto kore bolun.

Jader website nai: design pathan

  1. Website prompt copy kore Claude e paste korun. Claude oder business er tottho diye website design kore dibe.
  2. Email e "First email" pathan: free design er offer.
  3. Reply dile design er screenshot ba link "Send design" email e pathan.

Follow-up mone rakha

Card er Contact row theke Gmail, WhatsApp, SMS ba Messenger e pathale status nijei "Contacted" hoye jay (ba nije bodlan). 3 din por card ta upore chole ashe ar "follow-up due" dekhay. Follow-up due filter e shob ek sathe paben. Status shudhu ei browser e save thake.
